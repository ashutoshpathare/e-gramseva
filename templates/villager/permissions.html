Building Permission

Official Gram Panchayat construction permission for new buildings, extensions, or renovations. Required before commencing any construction work on residential or commercial plots.

  • Site plan / architectural drawing (PDF or image)
  • Applicant's identity proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID)
  • Proof of plot ownership

Water Connection Permission

New domestic or commercial water connection from the Gram Panchayat supply network. Required for first-time connections and pipe-size upgrades.

  • Property ownership proof / rent agreement
